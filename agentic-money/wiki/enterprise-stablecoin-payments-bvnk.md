# Enterprise Stablecoin Payments Infrastructure (BVNK)

**Source:** BVNK homepage, bvnk.com
**URL:** https://bvnk.com/

Product and company overview for [[bvnk]] — the enterprise stablecoin payments layer being acquired by [[mastercard]] for $1.8B.

## Scale

- **4,600+ stablecoin holders** as customers
- **130+ countries** supported for onboarding

## Two core products

### Managed Payments
BVNK provides licensing, custody, and compliance. Customers launch quickly without building compliance stack themselves.

### Layer1 (Self-Managed)
Customer brings own licenses, custodian, and liquidity partners. BVNK provides the underlying stablecoin payments network. Designed for enterprises that want to run their own stablecoin payments network in-house.

## Licensing stack

| License | Jurisdiction | Scope |
|---------|-------------|-------|
| VASP registration | Spain | Crypto |
| EMI licences | Malta + UK | Fiat |
| MSB + MTLs | Full US coverage | Fiat + Crypto |

## Notable customers (testimonials)

**Worldpay** — stablecoin payout service for marketplaces, travel, gaming clients; merchants receive stablecoin payouts without handling digital assets themselves.

**dLocal** — cross-border payment acceleration via BVNK stablecoin capabilities.

**Deel** — freelancers in 100+ countries opting to be paid in stablecoins since BVNK launch.

## Strategic significance

BVNK represents the enterprise stablecoin stack targeting the traditional payment processing market: large processors (Worldpay), cross-border specialists (dLocal), and global payroll (Deel). The Mastercard acquisition at $1.8B validates this segment.

The Layer1 product — enabling enterprises to run their own stablecoin payment network — is the most strategically interesting: it means large payment processors could offer stablecoin rails as a feature of their own product, without BVNK branding visible to end customers.
