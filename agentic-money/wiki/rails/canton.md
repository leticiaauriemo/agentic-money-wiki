# Canton

**Type:** Institutional blockchain / permissioned network
**One-line:** An institutional-grade blockchain network designed for regulated entities (banks, asset managers) that need onchain programmability and privacy without surrendering their compliance frameworks.

## How it works

Canton is positioned in the "institutional networks" category — a third blockchain archetype distinct from:
1. General-purpose chains (Ethereum, Solana) — open, public
2. Payments-specific chains (Tempo, Circle Arc) — optimized for payment throughput, stablecoin-native

Canton serves regulated entities that legally cannot participate in public, permissionless networks but want to benefit from programmable settlement, automated compliance, and cross-institutional coordination.

## Settlement speed & cost

Designed for institutional settlement workloads; specific benchmarks not published in this source.

## Who controls it

Canton is a Digital Asset Holdings product. Originally developed as the settlement layer for Goldman Sachs, BNP Paribas, and other institutional participants. As bank and asset-manager adoption of onchain infrastructure accelerates, this category is expected to grow significantly.

## Agent-friendliness

Moderate — programmable enough for agent-driven workflows within the compliance constraints of regulated institutions. Agents operating in institutional contexts (prime brokerage, custody, clearing) are the most natural users.

## Regulatory status

Designed for regulatory compliance; permissioned network structure allows KYC/AML enforcement at the network layer as well as smart contract layer.

## Who is building on it

- Digital Asset Holdings — creator
- Goldman Sachs, other institutional banks — early adopters
- Institutional asset managers (increasing adoption per a16z, 2026)
