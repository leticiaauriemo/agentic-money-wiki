# Agentic Banking: How AI Agents Are Transforming Finance

**Source:** [Backbase Blog](https://www.backbase.com/blog/agentic-banking)
**Publisher:** Backbase

## Key stats

- **McKinsey:** Agentic AI can return **10–12 hours/week** to each banker via multi-tool access
- **McKinsey:** Generative AI could add **$200B–$340B annually** to banking revenue
- **Deloitte:** **20–40% savings** in software investments for banking by 2028
- **86%** of financial services AI adopters say AI will be very/critically important in the next 2 years

## Core distinction

**Conversational AI** (current state): chatbots following decision trees, reactive, answers questions.

**Transactional AI / Agentic banking** (the future): understands intent, plans solutions, executes multi-step financial tasks without human direction.

## What blocks agentic banking today

**Fragmented legacy systems** — not technology readiness. Technology is available; integration across siloed data systems is the constraint. Unified data foundation + semantic layer is prerequisite.

"Bounded context" via semantic ontology constrains AI to safe banking concepts — the governance solution for preventing agents from acting outside their domain.

## Use cases by department

- **Customer service** — autonomous resolution, not just response drafts
- **Loan origination** — minutes vs. weeks
- **Fraud detection** — behavioral reasoning beyond rule-based systems
- **Compliance automation** — monitoring, flagging, reporting
- **Personalized financial guidance** — real-time, context-aware

## Implementation timeline (Backbase framing)

- Unified platforms: weeks to deploy agentic capabilities
- Fragmented legacy systems: months to years of integration work first

## Backbase's angle

This is vendor content positioning Backbase's platform as the unified foundation for agentic banking. The McKinsey/Deloitte statistics are credible; the framing is self-serving.
