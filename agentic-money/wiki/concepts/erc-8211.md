# ERC-8211

**One-line:** An April 2026 Ethereum standard (co-developed by Biconomy and the Ethereum Foundation) that adds dynamic, constraint-guarded execution to agent workflows — letting agents adapt multi-step DeFi strategies to live onchain conditions in real time.

## How it works

ERC-8211 extends the EVM agentic stack with **dynamic execution**: rather than a fully pre-specified transaction, an agent can operate within defined constraints and adapt its steps based on live onchain state (prices, pool liquidity, gas costs). The execution remains provably within authorized bounds even as the path adapts.

## Why it matters for agentic money

Yield optimization and DeFi strategies are inherently dynamic — a path through Aave, Uniswap, Chainlink, and Morpho in a single transaction chain depends on conditions at execution time. ERC-8211 makes such strategies available to autonomous agents without requiring humans to pre-specify every step for every state.
