# 9 Charts on What Stablecoins Are Becoming

**Source:** @a16zcrypto on X, 2026-04-25
**Authors:** Robert Hackett (features editor, a16z crypto), Jeremy Zhang (engineering partner, a16z crypto)
**URL:** https://x.com/a16zcrypto/status/2048070831264383301

Data-driven analysis of stablecoin usage trends through Q1 2026. The headline finding: stablecoins are becoming local payment infrastructure, not just cross-border tools.

## Key data points

### Volume
- **Q1 2026 adjusted volume: ~$4.5 trillion** — GENIUS Act accelerated growth that was already rising
- **$350–550B estimated true payments** (after stripping trading, treasury flows, exchange mechanics)
- Europe: post-MiCA baseline settled at $15–25B/month (up from near-zero in non-USD stablecoins)

### Transaction types (2025)
| Category | Count | YoY Change |
|----------|-------|------------|
| C2C (consumer-to-consumer) | 789.5M | — |
| **C2B (consumer-to-business)** | **284.6M** | **+128%** (from 124.9M in 2024) |
| B2B (by volume) | Dominant | — |

C2B is the fastest-growing category by transaction count — stablecoin commerce is accelerating.

### Velocity
- Stablecoin velocity (adjusted monthly transfer volume / circulating supply) **doubled from 2.6x to 6x** since early 2024
- Rising velocity = demand for transactions outpacing new issuance; supply working harder
- This is the signature of a **real payments network**, not a store-of-value asset

### Geography
- **Asia: ~2/3 of volume** (Singapore, Hong Kong, Japan dominate)
- **North America: ~1/4**
- **Europe: ~13%**
- Latin America + Africa: < $1B combined

### Cross-border vs. intra-country (the surprising trend)
- Cross-border share is **declining**, not rising
- Intra-country share grew from **~50% → ~75%** of payment volume (early 2024 → early 2026)
- Stablecoins are finding footing as **local payment medium running on global infrastructure**

### Stablecoin card infrastructure
- Rain-powered stablecoin card collateral (Etherfi Cash, Kast, Wallbit, others):
  - Near zero, November 2024
  - **$300M+/month by early 2026**

### Local currency stablecoins
- **BRLA** (Brazilian real-backed): near zero early 2023 → **~$400M/month by early 2026**; driven by PIX integration
- Non-USD stablecoins growing globally (MiCA drove euro-backed demand in Europe)

## The counterintuitive thesis

> "Many people thought stablecoins would be all about cross-border transactions. Instead, they're becoming more local."

The USD still dominates backing, but:
1. Non-USD local-currency stablecoins (BRLA, euro-backed) are gaining ground
2. Usage is shifting from cross-border to domestic payments
3. P2P transfers still dominate by count, but commerce is the fastest-growing segment

## Implications for agentic payments

- The "stablecoin for cross-border agent payments" thesis is only part of the story
- The bigger near-term opportunity may be **domestic B2B and C2B commerce rails**
- Velocity doubling means the infrastructure is being stressed — and being used, not just held
