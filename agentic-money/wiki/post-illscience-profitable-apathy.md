# Post by @illscience — Profitable Apathy

**Source:** [@illscience (Anish Acharya) on X](https://x.com/illscience/status/2048749482347745564), published 2026-04-27
**Author:** Anish Acharya, GP at a16z

## The thesis

Consumer financial services profits are built on **profitable apathy** — the asymmetry between a poorly-informed, friction-averse consumer and a sophisticated institution that exploits that friction. Agents destroy this asymmetry systematically.

> "The industry that brought you the efficient market depends on an inefficient consumer to eat."

---

## How agents dismantle consumer FS

**Exploit every customer subsidy:** Transfer bonuses, teaser rates, yield maximization, opening/closing accounts — agents will do this continuously and optimally.

**Route around fees:** Late fees, interest charges, overdrafts, expiration of teaser rates, mispriced debt that can be refinanced. Moving people from expensive debt to cheaper debt they're already approved for could save US families thousands per year.

**Bypass friction moat:** The industry has defended profit pools with asymmetric bureaucratic warfare — paperwork, forms, hold times, UI friction. Agents cut through this "like a hot knife through butter." The original industry defense against Plaid (blocking APIs) will be circumvented by agents navigating the same UIs humans use.

---

## The end state

A **headless credit auction**: every time you swipe your credit card, some lender bids on taking the risk and capturing the profit from that transaction.

Much more efficient. Much better for consumers. Significant contraction for many pockets of consumer financial services.

**The incumbent response:** VPs and managers will protect shrinking revenue lines the same way carriers protected SMS revenue as it collapsed. "They have zero chance of sticking the landing on new technology." The smart ones will pursue regulatory capture into the agentic economy.
