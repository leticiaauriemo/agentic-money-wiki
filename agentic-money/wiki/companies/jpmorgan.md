# JPMorgan Chase

**One-line:** Running $5B+ in average daily blockchain payment volume through Kinexys while cautiously watching stablecoins — building tokenized deposit infrastructure and calling out making APIs "AI-agent friendly" as a strategic priority.

## What they're building

JPMorgan's agentic/blockchain payments strategy has two tracks:

**Kinexys (formerly Onyx):**
- Blockchain-based deposit accounts, tokens, and securities
- JPM Coin: rolled out to institutional clients late 2025, launched on public chain Base early 2026, planning Canton network support
- Supports 24/7 settlement for large institutional payments

**API Agent-Friendliness:**
- Goldman Sachs has named this publicly; JPMorgan is pursuing same direction
- Not yet disclosed exactly what "AI-agent friendly" means in their implementation

JPMorgan is cautious on stablecoins specifically: stated they haven't seen wholesale demand yet, but remain "open to working on them."

## Relevance to agentic money

JPMorgan and Citi are the institutional banking counterpart to the fintech-led agentic payments wave. They're building tokenized deposit infrastructure that competes with stablecoins for large corporate payments — arguing that deposit tokens better integrate compliance infrastructure (KYC/AML) that lightweight stablecoin issuers may lack.

## Key stats

- **Kinexys:** over **$3 trillion** in volume; average daily transactions exceeding **$5 billion**
- JPM Coin: institutional rollout late 2025, now on Base (public chain)
- J.P. Morgan Payments: working with agents, networks, and participants as standards evolve
