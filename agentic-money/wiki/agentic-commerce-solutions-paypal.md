# PayPal Agentic Commerce Solutions for Businesses

**Source:** PayPal AI marketing site, paypal.ai
**URL:** https://www.paypal.ai/

Business-facing marketing overview of PayPal's agentic commerce positioning and product suite.

## Core positioning

> "Built for payments. Built for growth. Built for agentic."

PayPal frames its 25+ years of payments infrastructure as a trust moat for agentic commerce: fraud protection, identity, Purchase Protection, and Seller Protection — all carried into AI shopping experiences.

## The value proposition

**For merchants:**
- Sell across AI channels (major + emerging AI platforms)
- Single integration → multiple AI channels ("Connect once. Scale easily.")
- Maintain existing customer relationships (recognize returning customers)
- Decades of fraud protection applied to AI transactions

**The two-product stack:**
- **Agent Ready** — payments foundation (standalone or paired with Store Sync)
- **Store Sync** — product catalog + cart operations for AI discovery

> "Agent Ready is your payments foundation. Use it standalone or pair it with Store Sync to orchestrate and simplify the agentic commerce experience."

## AI partners shown

- **Perplexity** — agentic commerce surface
- **Google** — agentic commerce surface

These are confirmed live integrations visible in the marketing carousel.

## The trust moat argument

PayPal's strongest asset in agentic commerce is consumer trust, not technology. Per [[earning-consumer-trust-summary]] (Visa Research 2025): PayPal is one of the three most trusted brands for AI agent payments (42%, tied with Amazon and Visa).

The marketing page leans into this: 25 years of fraud protection + identity + Purchase/Seller Protection are presented as the reason to use PayPal's agentic infrastructure rather than crypto-native alternatives.

## Key tension

PayPal (via Cymbio acquisition) is betting on preserving the merchant-of-record model and traditional trust stack in agentic commerce. Crypto-native players ([[x402]], [[mpp]], [[coinbase]]) are betting on stablecoins and eliminating the merchant-of-record model entirely. The two approaches are not yet directly competing at scale, but they will be.
