# Fintech Frank Thread: Visa Bridges Crypto and Traditional Finance for Agents

**Source:** [X thread by @fintechfrank](https://x.com/fintechfrank/status/2028125642374492444)
**Author:** Frank Chaparro
**Published:** 2026-03-01

## Core observation

Visa is bridging crypto and traditional finance by supporting agentic commerce and stablecoin infrastructure simultaneously. Incumbent payment networks aren't being disrupted — they're adapting faster than expected.

## Visa stats cited

- **$4.6B annualized stablecoin volume**
- Stablecoin cards active in **50+ countries**
- USDC settlement live in US
- Agentic commerce live in US and CEMEA (Central/Eastern Europe, Middle East, Africa), expanding globally

## Startups attracting investor attention (triggered by thread)

The thread catalyzed significant venture interest in agentic payment startups:
- **NaturalPay** — "Payments for AI agents" (22 new investor follows in 7 days)
- **Archer Money** — "AI CFO" (YC S26; 5 new investor follows in 48 hours)
- **PayWithLocus** — "Make agents pay"
- **Trac Network** — agent payment infrastructure
- **RainCards** — agent payment cards
- **Purch** — bridges Visa liquidity with 1B+ products
- **TIBBR** — integration expected by end of March 2026

## Key framing

"The real competitive edge isn't novelty but reliability." Two layers of agentic commerce:
1. **Transaction layer** — Visa, card rails, settlement
2. **Intelligence layer** — ensuring purchases are right for users

## Significance

This thread shows the venture community response to agentic payments becoming real: a wave of startups targeting specific niches (AI CFO, agent cards, merchant bridging). Many of these companies are not yet in the wiki.
