# Mastercard Advances Agentic Payments in Latin America

**Source:** [Mastercard Press Release, 2026-03-23](https://www.mastercard.com/news/latin-america/en/newsroom/press-releases/pr-en/2026/march/)

## What happened

Mastercard executed live, end-to-end agentic payment transactions across Latin America and the Caribbean (LAC) — AI agents initiated and completed payments on behalf of customers using **existing Mastercard network infrastructure**, with full cardholder consent.

Transactions tested across: makeup, beauty accessories, groceries, books, digital goods.

**Key fact:** Nearly 100% of issuers in LAC were already enabled with Mastercard's agentic token technology before this pilot — making the region uniquely positioned for rapid adoption. No new rails or credentials were required.

## 16+ participating institutions

BAC, Banco de la Nacion Argentina, Banco Falabella, Banco Galicia, Banco Itaú, Banco Ripley, Banco Security de BICE, Bancolombia/Cibest Group, Banamex, Cencosud-Scotiabank, Davivienda, Dock, Evertec, Oriental Bank, Pomelo, Santander, ueno bank

## Technology used

- **Mastercard Agent Pay** — full platform
- **Agentic Tokens** — credentials bound to specific agent
- **Payment Passkeys** — authentication
- **Verifiable Intent** — tamper-resistant authorization record (co-developed with Google)
- **Agent Pay Acceptance Framework** — merchant integration standard

## Significance

This is the clearest evidence that agentic payments can scale on **existing card rails** without requiring new protocols (x402, MPP). Mastercard's approach: extend existing network with agent-specific trust and credentialing layers. Different philosophy from crypto-native approaches.
