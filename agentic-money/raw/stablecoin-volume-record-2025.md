# Stablecoin Transactions Rose to Record $33 Trillion in 2025

**Source:** Web search summary (unverified against primary source)
**Clipped:** 2026-04-27

## Key claims

- Stablecoin transaction volume reached **$33 trillion in 2025**, up **72% YoY**
- Stablecoins settle in seconds, 24/7 — no banking hours, no clearing windows
- High-frequency trading bots and DeFi protocols "flip" the same dollar thousands of times a day, which inflates gross volume figures significantly above true peer-to-peer or commerce payments volume

## Notes

The $33 trillion figure is gross on-chain volume. Adjusted volume (stripping HFT, DeFi recycling, treasury flows, exchange mechanics) is considerably lower — a16z estimated ~$4.5T in Q1 2026 alone in adjusted terms, and $350–550B as estimated true payments. The gap between gross and adjusted is the velocity story: the same stablecoin dollar does much more work per day than a traditional dollar sitting in a bank account.
