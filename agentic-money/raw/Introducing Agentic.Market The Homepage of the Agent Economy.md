Agentic commerce is here. The x402 ecosystem has experienced 165M+ transactions, ~$50M in volume, and ~100k services selling to 480k+ agents.

But discovery and reporting haven't kept pace. Until now, builders and agents relied on fragmented sources and word-of-mouth. Today, we’re launching [Agentic.Market](https://agentic.market/), the storefront for discovering, comparing, and using x402 services.

**What is** [Agentic.Market](https://agentic.market/)**?**

[Agentic.Market](https://agentic.market/) is a store designed for humans and their agents. It turns a fragmented technical ecosystem into a high-visibility, data-rich marketplace.

- **For Builders:** Gives your agent superpowers with a single place to evaluate and instantly use x402 enabled services.
- **For Providers:** Drives distribution to agent buyers through curation, enriched listings and real usage data.
- **For Agents:** Provides a machine-readable discovery layer to find and connect to services at runtime.
- **For Enterprises:** Offers a polished, data-rich view of the agentic commerce landscape.

**Core Features**

- **Zero Friction:** No API keys. No accounts. No logins.
- **Semantic Search:** Instant discovery across thousands of Bazaar services, including curated options across Inference, Data, Media, Search, Social, Infrastructure, and Trading.
- **Live Performance Metrics:** Real-time data on TPV, transactions, unique buyers/sellers and average value per transaction (by service).
- **Detailed Service Cards:** Everything needed to make a purchase decision and have your agent execute.
- **Automatic Indexing**: Services are picked up upon first use.

**Built for Humans and Agents**

We’ve designed two distinct ways to interact with this data:

1. **The Web Interface:** A sleek, filterable UI for humans to browse and evaluate services.
2. [The MCP](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/x402/bazaar)**:** Programmatic access for AI agents. Agents can now search, filter, and integrate new capabilities autonomously at runtime without a human in the loop.

**Why It Matters**

The agentic economy needs an adaptive schelling point for commerce. That's what we're committing to building with [Agentic.Market](https://agentic.market/).
