Now it's an archipelago of paywalls. LinkedIn, Google Maps, contacts, news — the data that really matters for your business is locked away.

When you ask a standard AI agent to find a lead or research a market, it gets stuck at a toll booth. It gives up or guesses. Brings you back something that looks solid — but isn't.

## Poncho gives your AI agent a wallet.

Not a corporate credit card. Not a $10,000 enterprise subscription. Just a little bit of agent cash to get past the toll booth. Two cents for a profile. Two cents for a premium search.

An all-access pass for the internet that gets you the data you actually need.

When Poncho hits locked data or tools, it pays a few cents for admission. No manual logins or expensive subscriptions.
