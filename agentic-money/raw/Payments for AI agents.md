Products

## Natural powers every type of agentic payments workflow

#### Wallet

Create FDIC-insured accounts that agents can use to hold, receive, and send funds

#### Pay

Enables agents to pay in a single simple tool call while Natural handles the rest of the complexity

#### Collect

Collect funds from customers, vendors, and agents via payment links, over the phone, or directly

#### Credit

Give agents access to credit so they can spend autonomously without funding

#### Bill

Allows agents to bill customers based on outcomes, success, tokens, or other defined criteria

#### Transfer

Power agentic transfers between Natural and external accounts of all types
