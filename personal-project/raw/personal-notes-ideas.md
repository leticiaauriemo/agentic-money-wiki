The key idea is that Transactional ecosystems were built around markets, and a market, when you boil it down to the bare bones, is a mechanism for trading property rights, as in “I sell you my shoes, in exchange for your money.” Trade in property rights allowed innovation to be a distributed process. No single person had to envision every possible improvement to any given technology. No single person had to understand how many different technologies, from different domains of knowledge and invented in different societies, could be made to fit together. No single person had to understand the structure of demand for a final product that might not yet exist. Rather countless individuals, following their self-interest could work it out incrementally, each taking price signals from the market and each one focusing on only the problem or task that directly concerned him or her.[[i]](#_edn1)
