## What they're building

MPP (Machine Payments Protocol) is Stripe's answer to agent-native commerce. Allows an agent to authenticate, authorize, and settle payments without friction — designed for the high-frequency, low-value payment patterns that agent workflows require.

## Relevance to this startup

MPP is payment infrastructure we would build on top of, not compete with. Our platform needs to settle payments between client agents and specialist agents; MPP (or x402) is a natural rail for that. The question is whether Stripe tries to expand MPP into a managed service layer.

## Open questions

- What does MPP offer beyond payment rails — any identity, routing, or governance layers?
