## What they're building

Letta (formerly MemGPT) provides a framework for building agents with persistent in-context and archival memory. Focuses on the architectural problem of agents maintaining coherent state over long interactions or across sessions.

## Relevance to this startup

Letta directly addresses part of the memory wall problem. However, their approach is in-org (one organization's agents managing their own memory). They don't address cross-org trust, discovery, or per-client context accumulation across a managed specialist network.

## Open questions
