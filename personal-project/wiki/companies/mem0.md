## What they're building

Mem0 gives AI agents the ability to remember facts, preferences, and context across conversations and sessions. Integrates with major agent frameworks. Useful for per-user or per-agent memory within one org.

## Relevance to this startup

Mem0 solves in-org per-agent memory. We solve per-client context across a curated specialist network. Orthogonal today — Mem0 could be used as a memory substrate inside our platform for specialists or for accumulating client context. They are not solving the trust, verification, or cross-org problems.
