## What they're building

Agentic.market is Coinbase's entry into the agent economy: a public marketplace for discovering and paying AI agents using the x402 protocol (HTTP 402 with crypto micropayments). Positioned as open infrastructure, not a managed service.

## Relevance to this startup

Coinbase is building the open public layer — discovery registry + payment rails. We are building the enterprise trust layer that sits on top of it. This is a complementary relationship: our specialists could be listed on Agentic.market, but clients using our service get the additional layers (vetting, sandboxing, per-client context, verification) that the open market doesn't provide.

Their launch validates the market but also raises the question: does an open market with good enough reputation signals eventually substitute for our managed service?

## Open questions
