# Open Questions

Living list. Every session either answers one or adds one.
When a question is answered, move it to [[resolved-questions]] or
integrate the answer into the relevant concept/analysis page.

---

## Product

- [ ] How much decomposition does the orchestrator do vs. letting the
      client's agent specify the breakdown?
- [ ] When a specialist fails verification: retry, switch, or escalate?
- [ ] Do we offer deterministic workflows alongside reasoning agents
      for high-stakes repeatable jobs?
- [ ] One verifier or a marketplace of verifiers?
- [ ] What does the client integration look like — SDK, API, or something
      the client's existing agent calls via MCP?
- [ ] How do we handle partial output — does the client see intermediate
      results or only final verified output?

---

## Market

- [ ] Who is the exact first customer? (Working hypothesis: Director/VP
      at a top-tier management consulting firm or policy research institution)
- [ ] Management consulting vs. policy/think tank as the very first pilot?
- [ ] Curated specialist network from day one, or controlled opening later?
- [ ] Do we seed with in-house-built specialists, or partner from the start?
- [ ] What does the sales motion look like — bottoms-up (agent developer
      adopts) or top-down (enterprise buys outcome)?

- [ ] Who is the right technical co-founder profile?
- [ ] Which advisor pathway: Hall → Mirhoseini, or Duffie → Athey?
- [ ] What is the minimum credible demo that gets a first pilot signed?
