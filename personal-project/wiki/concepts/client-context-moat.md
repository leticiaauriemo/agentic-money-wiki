# Client Context Moat

**One-line:** The compounding competitive advantage from accumulated per-client preferences, constraints, specialist fit, and workflow patterns — a new entrant starts at zero; the incumbent starts knowing the client.

**TODO: expand from sources**

## What accumulates over time

- Preferred deliverable formats (structure, depth, citation style)
- Standing constraints (jurisdictions covered, conflicts of interest, regulatory scope)
- Which specialists performed well for this client (and on which job types)
- Which outputs the client accepted vs. quietly rejected or revised
- Review and approval workflow patterns
- Budget and deadline norms

## Why this is a moat

A new entrant with a better underlying model still starts at zero context for each client. After 12 months on our platform, every new job starts with years of client-specific calibration already embedded in routing, specialist selection, and output formatting.

## Risks

- **Data portability pressure** — clients may demand export of their context profile
- **Model improvement curve** — if foundation models get dramatically better, raw capability may outweigh accumulated context
- **Key specialist relationships** — if a client's favorite specialists leave the network, context value drops
