“We’re doing a lot of machine-to-machine payments.”

Brian Armstrong @brian\_armstrong founder of Coinbase on giving AI agents stablecoin wallets.

“If you're an agent trying to get work done you either have to bug your human every time:

"Will you approve this purchase?"

Or you can tell the agent just go do this and the work will get done.

The AI agents might need to spin up AWS resources or get through a paywall on the internet to read some research paper or buy a domain name or spin up a marketing program etc..

If you really want to treat them like digital employees, they need to have a corporate card kind of thing and traditional corporate cards can't be issued to non-human entities, and so we're giving agents stablecoin wallets.

This is all very new in the last few months, but it's been getting a lot of traction.”
