### Things that used to take 20 clicks.

With Meow MCP, your AI agent handles the heavy lifting. One prompt, real answers.

Morning Cash Briefing

"Give me a morning briefing" — your agent pulls balances across every account, flags pending transactions, summarizes yesterday's outflows, and highlights any outlier charges worth reviewing. No login required.

Vendor Due Diligence

"Look up Basewell in our contacts" — your agent finds the saved payment contact, checks outstanding bills, validates their routing number against the Fed database, and confirms ACH capabilities before you send a dime.

Ride-Share Spend Audit

"How much did we spend on Uber and Lyft today?" — the agent scans card transactions and totals it up instantly. Five rides, $284, from three different employee cards. All in one answer.

SaaS Subscription Tracker

"What software are we paying for?" — in a single day's transactions: Anthropic, Dropbox, Frame.io, Supermemory, Adobe. Your agent categorizes and totals them without you opening a spreadsheet.

Multi-Entity Overview

"Compare balances across all our entities" — if you manage multiple legal entities, your agent queries each one and gives you a consolidated view: checking accounts, investment accounts, and digital wallets across all of them.

Treasury Monitoring

"How are our investments?" — your agent checks Treasury Account, Commercial Paper, Liquid Treasuries, and IG Bond Fund balances. No switching between dashboards, no waiting for reports.
