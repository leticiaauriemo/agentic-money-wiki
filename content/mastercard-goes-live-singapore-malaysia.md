# Mastercard Goes Live with Agentic Payments in Singapore, Malaysia

**Source:** MSN Money / Electronic Payments International
**Author:** Aninda Chakraborty
**Published:** April 2026

## What happened

Mastercard launched live agentic payments in Singapore and Malaysia, with additional ASEAN markets expected. This follows the Europe (Feb 27, 2026) and Latin America (March 23, 2026) rollouts.

## Technology

**Mastercard Agent Pay** framework combining:
- **Agentic Tokens** — agent-specific tokenized credentials
- **Payment Passkeys** — cardholder authentication
- **Verifiable Intent** — tamper-resistant authorization record co-developed with Google
- **Agent Pay Acceptance Framework** — merchant integration standard

## ASEAN significance

- United Overseas Bank (UOB) is the lead banking partner in ASEAN
- Mastercard plans to open a **regional AI Center of Excellence (CoE) in Singapore** in 2026
- Region positioned as Mastercard's third major market to go live with agentic payments

## BVNK acquisition

Mastercard agreed to acquire **BVNK** (stablecoin payments infrastructure) for up to **$1.8 billion** — providing Mastercard with stablecoin settlement rails alongside its traditional card infrastructure.

## Security model

Emphasis on three layers: consumer authentication, verifiable intent, end-to-end auditability. All transactions generate tamper-resistant audit trails.
