# Catena Labs — AI-Native Financial Institution

**Source:** [catenalabs.com](https://catenalabs.com/)

## Core concept

Catena Labs positions itself as the first "AI-native financial institution" — a regulated entity designed specifically for AI agents to securely identify themselves and transact. Unlike banks built for humans that are adapting to agents, Catena is built for agents from the ground up.

## Problem statement

Legacy financial systems are unprepared for agents as financial actors:
- Slow and expensive
- Require human identity verification that agents can't provide
- No mechanism for agent-specific risk profiles or compliance monitoring

## What Catena builds

- Regulated financial institution for AI agents (not humans)
- **AI-native money**: regulated stablecoins as the primary asset class
- **Cryptographic enforcement** instead of human oversight
- Bridges to traditional banking rails for interoperability

## Risk model

AI-specific risk management and compliance as core differentiators. Rather than adapting KYC/AML frameworks built for humans, Catena builds compliance that natively handles agent identity, behavioral monitoring, and authorization scope.

## Founder

Sean Neville — co-founder of Circle (created USDC). His background building the world's largest regulated stablecoin directly informs Catena's approach to AI-native money.

## Relationship to USDC and Circle

Neville brings the Circle playbook (regulated stablecoins + compliance infrastructure) to the agent economy. Catena is the institutional layer between agents and financial systems that Circle built for the stablecoin layer.
