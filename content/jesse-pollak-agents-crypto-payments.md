# Coinbase's Jesse Pollak Says AI Agents Are the Next Big Wave for Crypto Payments

**Source:** [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/04/25/coinbase-s-jesse-pollak-says-ai-agents-are-the-next-big-wave-for-crypto-payments)
**Author:** Margaux Nijkerk
**Published:** 2026-04-25

## Key stats

- ~**$48 million** in x402 payment volume processed to date
- ~**95% of x402 transactions** occur on Base (Ethereum L2)
- x402 integrations expanding across AI providers, data platforms, travel services

## Jesse Pollak's thesis

Crypto infrastructure is the natural fit for agentic payments because agents need to "make a single API call or smart contract call and move money globally, instantly, basically for free."

The key to crypto mass adoption: **invisibility** — people experience crypto without knowing it's crypto. Agents paying in stablecoins don't care about the underlying rail; they care about speed and cost.

## Two near-term agentic futures

1. **Fully autonomous "zero-human" businesses** — agents as the primary economic actors (emerging now, rare)
2. **Human augmentation via AI agents** — the more prevalent near-term shift; agents execute tasks humans define

Pollak sees #2 as the dominant pattern before #1 scales.

## Integration partners mentioned

Microsoft, Google, Mastercard, Alchemy

## Relevance to agentic money

This is the most recent (April 25, 2026) authoritative statement on x402's status from its creator. The $48M volume figure and 95% Base concentration are current data points.

> **Note:** x402 dashboard shows $49.51M all-time volume; the $48M figure here is from the same approximate date and likely reflects the same dataset.
