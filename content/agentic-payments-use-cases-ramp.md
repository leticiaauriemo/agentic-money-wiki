# Agentic Payments Use Cases, Risks & How to Get Started — Ramp

**Source:** [Ramp Blog](https://ramp.com/blog/agentic-payments)
**Author:** Matt Angelosanto
**Published:** 2026-04-14

## Key stats

- AI token spend across Ramp customers: **13x increase** since January 2025
- Early AP automation adopters: **3.5x** more transactions coded automatically, **98% accuracy**
- Ramp Policy Agent: catches **7x more** out-of-policy spend; reduces manual reviews by **4–5 hours/week**
- Ramp Agents for AP: **7x fewer clicks** to process invoices
- **74%** of procurement leaders report their data isn't AI-ready (Gartner survey)

## "Human on the loop" model

Ramp's framework: agents don't ask for approval on every step. Humans set policies and monitor outcomes. Agents execute within policy constraints.

This is distinct from "human in the loop" (approval required per step) and "full autonomy" (no human oversight).

## Live use cases

**Automated accounts payable (AP):**
- Auto-code line items to GL accounts
- Check 60+ fraud signals
- Surface card-eligible bills for cashback
- Reduce processing to 7x fewer clicks

**Subscription optimization:**
- Identify redundant SaaS subscriptions
- Flag unused licenses for cancellation

**FX optimization:**
- Evaluate currency routing in real time

**Procurement:**
- 3-way invoice matching
- Early-pay discount evaluation

## Control framework

- Single-use virtual card tokens (via Visa Intelligent Commerce / Trusted Agent Protocol)
- Network-level spending enforcement (not prompt-level)
- Immutable audit trails with cryptographic hashing (SOX compliance)
- 60+ fraud signals per invoice

## Risks acknowledged

- Data quality: 74% of procurement leaders say data isn't AI-ready
- Authorization creep: agents requesting broader scope than needed
- Audit trail requirements for compliance
