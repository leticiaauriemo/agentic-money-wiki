# JPMorgan and Citi Square Off on the Next Payments Frontier

**Source:** [Bloomberg, 2026-04-20](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-20/jpmorgan-and-citi-square-off-on-the-next-payments-frontier)
**Author:** Emily Mason

## Core story

JPMorgan and Citigroup are taking slightly different paths toward the same destination — the tokenized, 24/7 settlement future. Both are building but each has a different bet on stablecoins:

- **JPMorgan:** Kinexys infrastructure ($3T+ volume, $5B+ daily), cautious on external stablecoins ("haven't seen wholesale demand yet"), but open. JPM Coin now on Base (public chain) — notable shift.
- **Citigroup:** More open to stablecoins; tokenized deposit service live across 5 markets; working on crypto custody (summer 2026). 500+ clients enabled, $1B average per day.

## Key distinction: Deposit tokens vs. stablecoins

Banks argue **deposit tokens** are better for large corporate payments because they integrate existing compliance infrastructure (KYC/AML). Stablecoin issuers may take a "lightweight" approach to KYC, lacking banks' compliance sophistication.

Counter-argument: Stablecoins' programmable and internet-native nature is better suited for agent-to-agent and high-frequency micropayments. Traditional payment volumes "pale in comparison" to the $33T stablecoin volume in 2025.

## Stats

- JPMorgan Kinexys: **$3 trillion+** in volume; **$5 billion+** average daily transactions
- Citigroup: **500+ clients** enabled; **$1 billion** average daily; live in US, UK, Ireland, Hong Kong, Singapore
- Both: volumes "pale in comparison" to traditional system trillions/day

## Significance for agentic money

These are the two largest US banks explicitly preparing for tokenized/agent-initiated payments. Neither is working on x402 or MPP directly, but their infrastructure (Kinexys, Citi Token Services) represents the institutional layer that sits behind agent payments at scale. The competition between them and fintech-led approaches (Stripe, Coinbase) will define who settles the large-value agent transactions.

## Quotes

- Umar Farooq (JPMorgan, Global Co-Head Payments): stablecoins haven't shown wholesale demand yet, but open to working on them
- Shahmir Khaliq (Citigroup, Global Head of Services): building toward 24/7 tokenized settlement
