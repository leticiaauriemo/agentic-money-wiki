# Sui / Mysten Labs

**One-line:** Layer-1 blockchain (Mysten Labs) building native stablecoin and financial infrastructure designed for AI agents and institutional investors.

## What they're building

- **Sui Dollar (USDsui)** — native stablecoin for the Sui ecosystem, issued by Bridge (a Stripe subsidiary); reserves distributed as income to ecosystem participants
- **Hoshi** — native layer connecting Bitcoin on-chain for DeFi deployment on Sui
- **DeepBook** — liquidity layer combining trade execution and price discovery; intended as the default settlement venue for agent transactions on Sui
- **Institutional privacy** — selective regulator data-sharing while keeping most transaction data private

## Relevance to agentic money

Sui represents the "alternative L1" bet in agentic payments: building financial infrastructure natively for agents as first-class customers at the blockchain level, distinct from the card-rails approach (Mastercard, Visa) and Ethereum/Base approaches (x402, MPP). The thesis: money should move as freely as digital messages in a machine-to-machine economy.
