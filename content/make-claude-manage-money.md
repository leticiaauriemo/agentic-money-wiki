# Make Claude Manage Your Money — Era

**Source:** [era.app](https://era.app/)

## What Era is

Era provides a personal MCP (Model Context Protocol) for memory and money — a layer that lets Claude, ChatGPT, or other AI assistants autonomously manage a user's finances without requiring constant instructions.

## Key concept: Agency vs. Agents

Era distinguishes between:
- **Agents** — AI that acts on demand when you prompt it
- **Agency** — AI that acts autonomously while you're not engaged

Era aims to provide the latter: money management that happens automatically, not just when you ask.

## Capabilities

- Autonomous transfers based on user-defined rules
- Overspending detection and correction
- Portfolio adjustments
- Works across any LLM (cross-platform MCP layer)

## Regulatory status

SEC-regulated for investment functionality — Era has navigated the regulatory framework required for autonomous investment management.

## Design model

Users define intent and constraints once; Era's agents execute continuously within those bounds. This is a "human on the loop" model (not "human in the loop") — users set policy, agents act within it.

## Relevance to agentic money

Era represents the consumer-facing agentic banking use case: not an agent managing a business account, but an agent managing a personal financial life. SEC regulation is a meaningful differentiator — it suggests Era is operating in the actual investment management space, not just spending automation.
