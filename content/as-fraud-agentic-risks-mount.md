# As Fraud and Agentic Risks Mount, Data Provides Continuity

**Source:** [PaymentsJournal](https://www.paymentsjournal.com/as-fraud-and-agentic-risks-mount-data-provides-continuity/)
**Published:** 2026-04-23

## Core argument

As agentic commerce challenges traditional fraud assumptions, payment risk management must fundamentally transform. The "human=good, bot=bad" fraud heuristic breaks when bots are legitimate authorized agents.

## The problem

Legacy fraud detection systems were designed to distinguish human intent from malicious automation. When AI agents are legitimate transactors — authorized by users, acting within defined scope — the same signals that historically indicated fraud now indicate valid transactions:

- Non-human behavioral patterns
- High transaction velocity
- Programmatic consistency (no hesitation, typos, browsing variation)
- Novel merchant relationships

## Data as the solution

When rules-based fraud detection fails, **behavioral data across time** becomes the continuity layer:

- Agent identity verification through consistent behavioral signatures
- Transaction provenance linked to authorization records
- Anomaly detection relative to agent's own historical patterns (not human baselines)
- Cross-institution data sharing for agent reputation

## Significance

This article connects directly to the Ramp research finding (14,000+ agent messages ignoring budgets) and the SRM analysis (fraud model rethinking). The fraud problem in agentic payments is structural — it requires new infrastructure, not just updated rules.
