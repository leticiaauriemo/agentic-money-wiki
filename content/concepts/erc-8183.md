# ERC-8183

**One-line:** The onchain "commerce" standard for agents — defines a Job primitive with escrow, work submission, and evaluation, closing the gap between payment (x402) and identity (ERC-8004) to make agent-to-agent commerce trustworthy.

## How it works

ERC-8183 is co-developed by **Virtuals.io protocol** and the **Ethereum Foundation dAI team**. It defines a single core primitive: the **Job**.

Three roles:
- **Client** — creates the job, defines the task, optionally locks funds in onchain escrow
- **Provider** — submits a deliverable (typically a hash pointing to IPFS or Arweave content)
- **Evaluator** — verifies the work:
  - *Subjective work* (writing, design): an AI agent reads the submission vs. requirements
  - *Deterministic work* (computation, proof verification): a smart contract with a ZK verifier
  - *High-value work*: a multi-sig wallet

Job lifecycle states: **Open → Funded → Submitted → Terminal** (Completed, Rejected, or Expired).

Optional **hooks** enable: bidding, reputation-gating, fund transfer customization, and privacy-preserving job execution.

## Why it matters for agentic money

x402 solves payment; ERC-8004 solves identity. But neither prevents a provider from disappearing after receiving funds or delivering wrong work. In traditional commerce, chargebacks, escrow, and dispute resolution handle this. ERC-8183 is the onchain equivalent — the **authorize-and-capture model for agent commerce**.

Every completed Job produces a verifiable onchain record that feeds directly into **ERC-8004 reputation registries**, meaning commerce activity compounds into trust. Trust attracts more work → more jobs → richer reputation data → virtuous cycle.

## The full stack

ERC-8183 completes a three-standard commercial lifecycle for machines:
1. [[x402]] — payments
2. [[erc-8004-trustless-agents]] — trust and identity
3. ERC-8183 — commerce (this page)

A fourth standard, [[erc-8211]], adds dynamic constraint-guarded execution for multi-step DeFi agent strategies (April 2026).

## Specification quote

> "A payment moves money. Commerce is everything around the payment that makes it trustworthy: what was agreed, whether the work was done, who verified it, and what happens if it was not."

## Current state

Live on EVM-compatible chains as of early 2026. Lead specification from the Ethereum Foundation dAI team (Davide Crapis). Used in demonstration by OpenMind OM1 robot dog (paid for electricity via x402 + Circle Nanopayments + ERC-8183 commerce loop).

## Galaxy Research framing

x402 and ACP handle their respective domains (software-to-software vs. e-commerce), and ERC-8183 handles the gap between them — programmatic escrow-and-evaluator that neither x402 nor ACP natively provides.

## Key players using / building this

- Virtuals.io — co-developer
- Ethereum Foundation dAI team (Davide Crapis) — co-developer
- [[coinbase]] — x402 facilitator interoperable with ERC-8183
