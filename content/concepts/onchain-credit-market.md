# Onchain Credit Market

**One-line:** The emerging second act of the stablecoin economy — as payment volume scales to trillions, the float creates demand for productive capital deployment, giving rise to onchain lending, working capital, and capital formation for underserved borrowers.

## How it works

Stablecoin issuers hold enormous float. Businesses holding stablecoin treasuries want to put capital to work. Protocols need liquidity. End users will eventually want to borrow. The convergence of these pressures creates a new credit layer built on programmable, onchain infrastructure.

This is structurally closer to productive credit (lending against real assets and receivables, working capital for businesses) than to early DeFi (crypto lent against crypto to speculate on crypto).

## Why it matters for agentic money

Payments is the first act: stablecoins unlock 24/7, programmable, sub-cent transactions for agents. Credit is the second: agents will eventually need to manage working capital, make credit decisions, and access liquidity. An onchain credit market is the natural complement to stablecoin payment rails.

The ordering matters: dollar access is the wedge (payments), then the full suite of financial products (credit, investing, wealth management, insurance) follows. Payments open the account; credit and investing build the business.

## The private credit analogy (a16z)

> "As banks retreated from certain lending categories under regulatory pressure, private credit funds filled the gap. They grew from a niche alternative asset class into a multi-trillion dollar market that now competes directly with syndicated lending. The onchain credit market is structurally similar: Capital forming outside the traditional banking system, in new structures, serving borrowers that the legacy system underserves. The difference is the infrastructure layer underneath, which is open, programmable, and global."

## Emerging market angle

In markets where local banking infrastructure fails — large parts of LatAm, sub-Saharan Africa, Southeast Asia — stablecoins first enable dollar-denominated payments (vendor payments, global collections, treasury management). The credit market follows as the existing dollar relationship deepens.

## Current state

Early-stage. Onchain lending primitives exist (Aave, Morpho, etc.) but are primarily crypto-collateralized. Real-world asset (RWA) lending is growing but nascent. The next phase — working capital and productivity credit for underserved businesses — is the anticipated next transition.

Traditional credit managers are beginning to take notice.

## Key players building this

- Protocol layer: Aave, Morpho (existing DeFi lending)
- RWA lending: multiple projects (not yet tracked in this wiki)
- Infrastructure bet: a16z crypto (thesis source)
