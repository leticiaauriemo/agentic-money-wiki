# Stablecoin Transactions Rose to Record $33 Trillion, Led by USDC

**Source:** Bloomberg, Suvashree Ghosh, 2026-01-08
**URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-08/stablecoin-transactions-rose-to-record-33-trillion-led-by-usdc

## Key findings

- **Total 2025 stablecoin volume:** $33 trillion gross, up **72% YoY**
- **USDC:** $18.3 trillion (led despite smaller market cap than USDT)
- **USDT:** $13.3 trillion (dominates market cap at $187B vs. USDC's $75B)
- **Q4 2025 alone:** $11 trillion (record quarter), up from $8.8T in Q3
- **2030 projection:** Bloomberg Intelligence estimates $56 trillion in stablecoin payment flows

## Why USDC leads in volume despite smaller market cap

USDC is the preferred stablecoin on DeFi platforms, which run high-frequency lending/trading. Same dollar of USDC reused many times. Tether CEO Paolo Ardoino's rebuttal: when isolating single-counterparty simple transfers, USDT held ~70% of volume — more representative of real-world payment behavior.

## Drivers

- Pro-crypto policy under Trump administration
- GENIUS Act (July 2025) providing legal clarity
- Institutional adoption: Standard Chartered, Walmart, Amazon exploring launches
- Trump family's World Liberty Financial launched USD1 stablecoin (March 2025)
- DeFi platform share of volume fell — suggesting mainstream adoption over speculative use

## IMF warning (October 2025)

The IMF warned the stablecoin market could threaten traditional lending, hamper monetary policy, and trigger runs on safe assets.
