# Visa Announces Global Expansion of Agentic Ready Program

**Source:** Visa Inc. Press Release via Business Wire, 2026-04-29
**URL:** https://usa.visa.com/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases.releaseId.22341.html

## Summary

Visa is expanding its Agentic Ready program from Europe/UK (where it launched with 20+ partners) to 85+ partners in Asia Pacific and Latin America. Program continues to roll out to additional markets through 2026.

## What Agentic Ready provides

Visa Agentic Ready is a program for issuing banks and payment partners to prepare for AI agent-initiated commerce. Participants can:

- Test agent-initiated payments in controlled, real-world environments with live cards and real merchants
- Validate core payment flows (card enrollment, tokenization, authentication, authorization)
- Assess trust, security, and control mechanisms as agents act for consumers/businesses
- Identify operational and readiness gaps before agent-led transactions scale broadly
- Collaborate with Visa and merchants to understand how agent-initiated transactions behave in practice
- Prepare for global deployment as agent-driven commerce expands

## Context

Agentic Ready builds on [[visa]]'s Intelligent Commerce portfolio. Already live in the US with broad agents and partners doing live transactions. The program is the testing/readiness layer for issuers and partners — not a protocol itself.

Quote from Rubail Birwadker (SVP, Growth Products & Partnerships): "Visa Agentic Ready provides banks and issuing partners with a structured path to testing agent-initiated payments, learning what works, and ensuring global readiness as these experiences reach scale."
