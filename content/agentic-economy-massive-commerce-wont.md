# The Agentic Economy Will Be Massive. Agentic Commerce Won't.

**Source:** [X/Twitter thread by @robbiepetersen_](https://x.com/robbiepetersen_/status/2036118525451047170)
**Author:** Robbie Petersen
**Published:** 2026-03-23

## Core thesis

**95%+ of agentic deployment over the next 5 years will be top-down within organizations, not autonomous merchant transactions.** The "agentic economy" is massive; "agentic commerce" (agents autonomously buying from the open web) is narrow.

## Three categories of agents

**1. Commercial agents (top-down, ~95%)**
Organizations deploy agents to automate tasks: sales, finance, legal, operations. These agents transact within the company's existing financial infrastructure (corporate cards, bank APIs). No need for crypto rails or new payment protocols.

**2. Consumer agents**
Agents help users research purchases, compare prices, draft communications. Humans still make final decisions. Consumer agents assist discovery but don't make autonomous purchases.

**3. Bottom-up autonomous agents (~5%)**
Genuinely autonomous entities operating outside organizational control. OpenClaw phenomenon catalyzed their emergence. These are the agents that need permissionless crypto rails because they have no organization backing them.

## Why commercial agents don't need crypto

- Companies negotiate volume agreements with compute providers (AWS, Azure, GCP), model providers (Anthropic, OpenAI), and data providers (Bombora, Clearbit) — bundled into single invoice lines
- Granularity of consumption ≠ granularity of settlement
- Agents can hold FBO (For Benefit Of) bank accounts under existing banking frameworks; no crypto wallet needed

## Why consumer agents won't buy autonomously at scale

- Preferences are revealed through choosing — consumers value the discovery and decision process itself
- Granting agents autonomous purchasing power means surrendering a source of meaning and agency

## Where crypto wins

Crypto's advantage isn't technical superiority — it's **permissionlessness**. Bottom-up autonomous agents default to crypto because they have no organization to provide traditional financial infrastructure. x402 and stablecoins are the path of least resistance, not the superior technology.

## Implication for rail competition

Card networks (Visa, Mastercard) win the 95% commercial use case. Crypto rails win the 5% bottom-up autonomous agent market. Both win, but the addressable markets are different sizes.
