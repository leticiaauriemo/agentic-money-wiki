# Agentic Commerce Won't Kill Cards, But It'll Open A Gap

**Source:** [X thread by @nlevine19](https://x.com/nlevine19/status/2029229792944636122)
**Author:** @nlevine19
**Published:** 2026-03-04

## Core argument

Stablecoins won't replace cards for existing merchants and consumers. Cards dominate because they offer credit, fraud protection, chargebacks, and rewards (82% of Americans carry rewards cards). Stablecoins' real opportunity is serving the merchants that incumbent payment processors cannot yet underwrite.

## The historical pattern

Every major platform shift creates merchants that incumbent systems can't serve:
- PayPal for eBay sellers (40% of eBay auction payments by 2000)
- Shopify for small businesses (42,000 → 5.5M merchants in 13 years)
- Stripe for startups

Traditional processors took 16 years to develop underwriting guidelines for PayFacs after PayPal launched.

## The new underserved merchant: "vibe coders"

- 36 million new developers joined GitHub last year
- YC Winter 2025: 25% of companies had codebases 95%+ AI-generated
- Bolt.new: 67% of 5 million users are not traditional developers
- These builders create 4-hour AI tools generating $40/week revenue — **with no legal entity, no website, no track record**

Traditional payment processors have no underwriting mechanism for these merchants. x402 enables stablecoin payments with no merchant account, no chargeback liability, no processor onboarding.

## The framing that matters

Not "stablecoins vs. cards" — but "**what merchants exist outside the traditional underwriting box?**"

These merchants choose stablecoins over nothing, not stablecoins over cards. When they eventually scale, they'll likely absorb card processing too (repeating the PayPal/Stripe pattern).

## Key stats

- 18 billion cards in circulation globally
- Visa has issued 16 billion tokens
- Visa has processed 2 billion transit fares (aggregated settlements)
