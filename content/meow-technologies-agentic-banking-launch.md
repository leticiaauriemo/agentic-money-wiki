# Meow Technologies Launches the First Agentic Banking Platform for AI Agents

**Source:** [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/meow-technologies-agentic-banking-ai-agents)
**Author:** Cristian Dina
**Published:** 2026-04-10

## What happened

Meow Technologies announced (April 8, 2026) what it calls "the world's first agentic banking platform" — allowing AI agents to autonomously open business bank accounts, issue cards, execute transfers, and manage invoicing without human intervention.

## Company background

- Founded 2021, San Francisco
- Originally launched as a corporate treasury platform for DeFi yield
- Evolved into full business banking platform
- Holds **over $1 billion** in assets
- Raised **~$30 million** from: Tiger Global, QED Investors, Lux Capital, Slow Ventures, Coinbase Ventures, Gemini Frontier Fund
- CEO: Brandon Arvanaghi

## How it works

MCP server at `meow.com/mcp` — any MCP-compatible agent connects natively (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Gemini).

**Default safety model:** Agents cannot transfer funds unilaterally. Every transfer requires initiator-and-approver workflow mirroring human employee authorization rules. Hard controls: two-factor auth, role-based permissions, transfer limits.

## Agent capabilities (via MCP)

- Open business bank accounts
- Issue virtual cards
- Execute ACH transfers
- Manage invoicing
- Configure spending limits and permissions

## Significance

This is the clearest case of a traditional banking product rebuilt for agents as the primary customer — not humans using agents, but agents as the account holder. The platform's permissioned architecture demonstrates that hard infrastructure controls (not agent self-regulation) are the correct design pattern.
