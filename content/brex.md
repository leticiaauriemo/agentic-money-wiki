# Brex

**One-line:** Fintech corporate card company adding agentic finance capabilities — AI agents that audit, review, and approve expenses, and a personal Brex Assistant that replaces manual expense reporting.

## What they're building

Brex calls its offering "Intelligent Finance" — using AI agents to eliminate the busywork of corporate spend management:

**Audit Agent:** Monitors company expenses against internal processes, categorizes violations by risk level, runs continuously without human initiation.

**Review Agent:** Auto-approves low-risk expenses, escalates exceptions, follows up on non-compliance, operates within company policy rules.

**Brex Assistant:** Personal AI assistant for employees that replaces manual expense reporting — employees no longer submit expenses, the agent does it.

Key claim: finance teams can be "multiplied without hiring" by training agents on expense policies and SOPs. Full auditability and transparency by design.

Quoted customer: Sophie Chiang (Head of GL Accounting, Plaid) — endorsed auto-categorization, memos, and AI suggestions.

## Relevance to agentic money

Brex represents the **internal enterprise agent** play — agents operating within a corporate financial system on behalf of employees, not consumers. This is the "commercial agents" category that analyst @robbiepetersen_ argues will be 95%+ of agentic deployments at scale. Brex is building the infrastructure that makes this possible for finance teams.
