# Post by @lightspark — Visa Stablecoin/Bitcoin Cards

**Source:** [@lightspark on X](https://x.com/lightspark/status/2049888918599065885), published 2026-04-30

## What launched

Lightspark + Visa partnership: **stablecoin and Bitcoin-backed Visa debit cards across 100+ countries**.

Through Lightspark's Grid platform, financial institutions, fintechs, and businesses can offer customers Visa debit cards funded by:

| Funding source | Notes |
|----------------|-------|
| USDC and other stablecoins | Solana, Base, and Spark |
| Bitcoin | Via Spark or Lightning Network |
| Fiat (USD, EUR) | Traditional |

Cardholders spend at **175M+ Visa-accepting merchants** worldwide. Bridges on-chain accounts to everyday purchases.

## Significance

This is the old rails meeting the new ones — a Visa card that settles from a crypto/stablecoin balance rather than a bank account. For Lightspark, it extends Grid beyond its MPP machine payments positioning into consumer spending.

For Visa: further confirmation that the network is positioning as the bridge layer between traditional card infrastructure and the crypto/stablecoin layer. Visa is simultaneously an anchor validator on Tempo, a participant in the x402 Foundation, and now a distribution partner for Lightspark's crypto-backed cards.
