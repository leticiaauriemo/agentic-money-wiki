# Tempo

**One-line:** A purpose-built stablecoin payments and issuance infrastructure company, and co-author of the Machine Payments Protocol ([[mpp]]).

## What they're building

Tempo provides infrastructure for stablecoin payments at the enterprise level — targeting treasury teams, financial institutions, and payment providers. Their stack covers stablecoin issuance, cross-border settlement, and programmable payment flows via smart contracts. They built the TIP-20 token standard, which adds transfer memos, compliance controls, and reward distribution on top of standard token functionality.

## Relevance to agentic money

Tempo co-authored [[mpp]] (Machine Payments Protocol) alongside Stripe. This makes them a foundational infrastructure player in the machine payments space — they're not just building on top of existing rails but defining one of the three main agentic payment protocols. Their explicit listing of "agentic commerce" as a first-class use case signals this is a core strategic bet.
