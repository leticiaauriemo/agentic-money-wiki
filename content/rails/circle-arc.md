# Circle Arc

**Type:** Payments-specific blockchain
**One-line:** Circle's purpose-built payments blockchain, competing with Stripe's Tempo chain on stablecoin-native gas fees, privacy guarantees, and predictable transaction costs for institutional fintechs.

## How it works

Circle Arc is a payments-specific Layer-1 blockchain optimized for financial services use cases that general-purpose chains (Ethereum, Solana) were never designed for:
- **Stablecoin-native gas fees** — payments denominated and settled in stablecoins, no need for a separate gas token
- **Privacy guarantees** — designed for financial transaction privacy (complementing Ethereum's Kohaku roadmap)
- **Predictable transaction costs** — essential for fintechs modeling costs at scale; variable gas is unworkable for payment products

## Settlement speed & cost

Purpose-built for high-throughput payments with predictable costs. Specific benchmarks not yet published; positioned as competitive with [[tempo]] (Stripe's payments chain) on these dimensions.

## Who controls it

[[Circle]] — USDC issuer and Circle Arc developer.

## Agent-friendliness

High — stablecoin-native gas removes the need for agents to hold a separate gas token, reducing friction for programmatic payments.

## Who is building on it

- [[circle]] — creator; USDC is the native asset

## Regulatory status

Not separately specified. Circle operates under existing US and EU regulatory frameworks (GENIUS Act compliance, MiCA). Arc's OCC National Trust Charter implications are linked to Circle's issuer-level charter pursuit.

## Competitive positioning

Arc competes directly with [[tempo]] (Stripe's payments chain) in the payments-specific blockchain category. Both prioritize:
- Privacy
- Predictable fees
- Stablecoin-native execution

General-purpose chains (Ethereum L2s, Solana) are the third competitor category for the same payment workloads.
