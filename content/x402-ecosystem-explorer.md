# x402 Ecosystem Explorer (x402scan)

**Source:** [x402scan.com](https://www.x402scan.com/) / [x402scan resources](https://www.x402scan.com/resources)

## What it is

x402scan is a live analytics dashboard and marketplace explorer for the x402 payment protocol. Tracks real merchant and buyer activity across agents using x402 for micropayments.

## Ecosystem stats (as of April 2026)

- **293** active merchants
- **344** servers listed as searchable resources
- **54** new merchants in recent measurement period
- **559** unique registered buyers
- **821** crypto-related servers
- **727** AI resource servers
- **303** trading information servers

## Market categories

Servers fall into: AI gateway/LLM access, DeFi data/trading, agent identity and commerce, enrichment APIs, niche services (sport court maps, gaming, creative tools).

## Significance

x402scan shows the real adoption picture — many servers are unnamed test endpoints (ngrok/Railway URLs), indicating rapid experimentation. Most volume is concentrated in a few high-activity servers, with ATXP dominating.
