# Profound

**One-line:** Analytics platform that tracks and optimizes how products appear in AI-native shopping results, starting with ChatGPT Shopping.

## What they're building

Profound monitors SKU-level product visibility inside AI answer engines (currently ChatGPT Shopping), giving brands dashboards on citation rates, keyword discovery, attribute accuracy, and merchant-layer checkout control. Their pitch is that "nearly eight in ten buyers now rely on answer engines for at least half of purchase decisions" — so the battleground for commerce is moving from SEO/ads into LLM output.

## Relevance to agentic money

Profound is infrastructure for the brand side of agentic commerce. As AI agents become the primary shopping interface for consumers, brands need to understand and influence how those agents retrieve and rank their products. Profound is the earliest category entrant building that visibility and optimization layer.

## Funding & traction

No public funding disclosed. Offers demo and freemium sign-up; login-gated product.

## Products / offerings

- **Shopping Visibility:** SKU-level tracking of appearance rate in ChatGPT Shopping results
- **Attribute Accuracy:** Monitoring of how AI categorizes and describes products
- **Shopper Sentiment:** Analysis of how AI-generated product descriptions affect buyer intent
- **Keyword Intelligence:** Reveals which consumer queries are driving discovery
- **Merchant Layer:** Shows which retailers control checkout options for your products
- **Competitive Analysis:** Identifies where competitor products surface but yours don't
- **Feed Optimization:** Structured data recommendations to improve catalog prioritization
