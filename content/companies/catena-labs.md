# Catena Labs

**One-line:** The "first AI-native financial institution" — built from scratch for AI agents as customers, not humans, by Sean Neville (co-founder of Circle and co-inventor of USDC).

## What they're building

Catena Labs is building financial infrastructure where **AI agents are the intended customers**, not humans. Unlike banks that reluctantly accommodate agents as edge cases, Catena is designed from first principles assuming agents are primary actors.

Core design philosophy: Instead of keeping bots out (traditional banking), Catena designs compliance, identity, and policy layers to let **the right agents in safely**, with cryptographic enforcement rather than human oversight.

Key components:
- **AI-specific risk management and compliance** — designed for agent transaction patterns, not human ones
- **AI-native money** (regulated stablecoins) — the account currency
- **Compliance infrastructure** — 100 AI agents doing compliance monitoring, overseen by just 2 humans (per podcast demo)
- **Bridges to traditional banking rails** — for interoperability with legacy systems
- **Policy engine with cryptographic enforcement** — agents operate within hard bounds, not soft guardrails

## Relevance to agentic money

Catena Labs represents the most radical institutional bet: that the coming wave of agent-initiated payments needs a purpose-built institution, not adaptations of existing banks. Sean Neville spent 13 years building USDC as infrastructure; Catena is his bet on what the agent economy needs next.

Sean's belief (stated in podcast): "Every financial transaction will eventually be executed by AI" — and Catena is designed to be the institution that handles that.
