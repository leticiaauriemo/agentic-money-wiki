# Sui Unveils Infrastructure for AI-Driven 'Agentic Finance'

**Source:** [Bloomingbit](https://en.bloomingbit.io/feed/news/110163)
**Author:** Doohyun Hwang
**Published:** 2026 (pre–June 17 Seoul press briefing)

## What Sui is building

Sui (L1 blockchain by Mysten Labs) is launching native stablecoin and financial infrastructure targeted at AI agents and institutional investors:

- **Sui Dollar (USDsui)** — native stablecoin for Sui, issued by Bridge (a Stripe subsidiary)
- **Hoshi** — native layer connecting Bitcoin on-chain for DeFi deployment
- **DeepBook** — liquidity layer combining trade execution and price discovery; intended as the default settlement venue for agent transactions on Sui

## Key details

- USDsui reserves distributed as income to ecosystem participants (not just held)
- Institutional privacy: selective regulator data-sharing while keeping most transactions private
- Adeniyi Abiodun (Chief Product Officer, Mysten Labs) presented at Seoul briefing June 17

## Core thesis

Money should move as freely as digital messages in a machine-to-machine economy. AI agents need native financial rails to hold and manage assets autonomously — traditional systems require human intermediaries that agents can't interact with.

## Relevance to agentic money

Sui represents the "alternative L1" bet in agentic payments: building financial infrastructure at the blockchain level for agents as first-class customers, distinct from the card-rails (Mastercard, Visa) and Ethereum/Base (x402/MPP) approaches.

## Notable: Bridge is a Stripe subsidiary

The USDsui issuer (Bridge) was acquired by Stripe, making this an indirect Stripe play in the Sui ecosystem.
