# Visa's Token Strategy: A Quiet Shift Powering Future Payments

**Source:** Zacks Equity Research via Yahoo Finance, 2026-03-16
**URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/news/visas-token-strategy-quiet-shift-174700458.html

## Key finding

Visa now has **more than 17.5 billion tokens circulating globally** — over **three times** the number of physical cards linked to its network. This is the primary infrastructure enabling AI-driven and agentic commerce.

## Why tokenization matters for agentic payments

Tokens replace sensitive card data with unique digital substitutes scoped to specific transactions or merchants. For agentic commerce specifically:

- **Automated purchases** depend on secure, programmable payment credentials
- **Embedded finance** and AI-driven shopping experiences require tokens that can be initiated across apps, devices, and digital agents without exposing underlying card details
- **Repeat purchases / subscriptions** benefit from tokens that boost approval rates when stored credentials need renewal
- **Fraud protection** — tokens prevent exposure of raw card data even when agents are unauthorized by a malicious actor

## Visa's long-term goal

Moving toward a **fully tokenized transaction environment** — redefining how digital payments are authenticated, secured, and scaled.

## Competitive context

- **Mastercard** (MDES — Mastercard Digital Enablement Service): similar tokenization for digital wallets, e-commerce, card-on-file
- **American Express** (AMEX Token Service): supports card-on-file, subscriptions, digital wallets

## Relevance to agentic payments

Visa's token strategy is the infrastructure layer underneath Intelligent Commerce, Agentic Ready, and Trusted Agent Protocol. Tokens are what Visa calls "Agentic Tokens" in the Agent Pay context — they scope spending authority and carry verifiable intent.
