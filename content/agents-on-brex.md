# Agents on Brex: Welcome to Intelligent Finance

**Source:** [Brex Platform](https://www.brex.com/platform/intelligent-finance)

## What Brex is building

Three AI agent types for corporate finance:

**Brex Assistant** — personal finance assistant for employees; handles expense categorization, receipt matching, policy guidance.

**Audit Agent** — monitors and categorizes company spending continuously; flags out-of-policy transactions automatically.

**Review Agent** — auto-approves compliant expenses and escalates exceptions to humans based on policy rules. Learns from feedback over time to improve precision.

## Core value proposition

AI agents eliminate expense management busywork by automating categorization, review, and approval of company spending based on internal policies — enabling finance teams to scale without hiring additional staff.

## Notable: Plaid as a Brex customer

Brex highlights Plaid as a customer of its intelligent finance products, suggesting B2B penetration among fintech companies themselves.

## Design philosophy

Transparency is a core design principle — agents show their reasoning and categorization logic. Policy-based automation means agents act within human-defined constraints (not open-ended autonomy).

## Relationship to Ramp

Both Brex and [[ramp]] are building agent-powered corporate finance products. Brex emphasizes employee-facing intelligence; Ramp focuses more on AP automation and accounting. Both represent the "top-down agentic payments" pattern.
