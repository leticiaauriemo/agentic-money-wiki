# Statistics on B2B Payments in 2026: Net30, Net60, and Digital Adoption

**Source:** ClearlyPayments / Kalle Radage, 2026-01-15
**URL:** https://www.clearlypayments.com/blog/statistics-on-b2b-payments-in-2026-net30-net60-and-digital-adoption/

## Market size

- **Global B2B payments:** $150T–$180T USD annually (2026) — **>85% of all global payment value**
- **Consumer payments:** ~$20T–$30T annually (fewer dollars, more transactions)
- North America: ~30–35% of global B2B value
- Cross-border B2B growing at 6–8% CAGR

## Net30/Net60 still dominate

- Net30 offered on **55–65% of North American B2B invoices**
- Net60 on 15–25% (enterprise-heavy)
- Only **52–58% of Net30 invoices paid on time**; 20–25% paid 1–30 days late

## DSO benchmarks

- SMBs: 38–45 days
- Mid-market: 45–55 days
- Enterprise: 55–65 days
- Businesses offering digital payment alternatives report 12–18 fewer DSO days

## Digital adoption

- 65–70% of B2B invoices now issued electronically
- 45–50% of B2B payments initiated digitally (ACH/EFT, wire, cards)
- Checks: still ~30–35% of count in North America, but <15% of value
- Automated invoicing reduces processing costs **60–80%**

## Credit card usage in B2B

- Cards: ~8–12% of B2B payment volume, 20–25% of transaction count (skewed toward smaller payments)
- Cards used in **>40% of B2B SaaS, IT, and digital marketing payments**
- Average B2B card transaction: $2,500–$7,000 depending on industry
- Card acceptance reduces DSO by **10–20 days**
- Invoices >$25K: paid via Net Terms >80% of the time
- Transactions <$10K: paid by card 35–45% of the time when offered

## Relevance to agentic payments

B2B is where agentic payments have the most immediate commercial opportunity — it's $150–180T/year vs. $20–30T in consumer, and it's the segment where:
- Settlement speed matters (slow DSO = cash flow risk)
- Cross-border friction is highest
- Agents could automate invoice matching, payment routing, and reconciliation at scale
- Stablecoins + x402/MPP could replace Net30 float with instant settlement
