# Agentic Payments: The Buzz Is Real, But So Is The Gap

**Source:** [Forbes Business Council](https://www.forbes.com/councils/forbesbusinesscouncil/2026/03/10/agentic-payments-the-buzz-is-real-but-so-is-the-gap/)
**Author:** Cole Dermott (CEO, Locus)
**Published:** 2026-03-10

## Core argument

The gap isn't lack of use cases — it's trust. Most AI agent applications still operate in rigid workflows (coding, legal research, customer support) and are not truly autonomous economic actors. The challenge is getting organizations to grant agents financial autonomy.

## Three gaps

1. **Identity verification** — no standard for verifying agent identity before granting spending power
2. **Delegated sub-accounts** — agents need accounts specifically designed for them, not shared access to human accounts with bolt-on controls
3. **Policy-driven permissions** — revocable access, spend limits, and merchant restrictions must be infrastructure-level, not prompt-level

## 3–5 year outlook

Dermott projects that within 3–5 years:
- Mature agentic payment systems with programmable flows will exist
- Machine-to-machine marketplaces will emerge
- **Agents may become a new class of internet users**, outnumbering humans in online transactions

## Why agents fail now

Regulatory frameworks weren't built for autonomous agents — clarity will emerge as technology matures. Trust hasn't kept pace with capability. Organizations are cautious about granting financial autonomy to systems they can't audit.

## Prescriptions

- Build financial infrastructure built specifically for agents (not adapted human infrastructure)
- Revocable access is mandatory
- Policy-driven permissions at the infrastructure level
