# Agentic Payments Are Coming: Moving from Concept to Competitive Issue

**Source:** [SRM Corporation](https://srmcorp.com/agentic-payments-are-coming-moving-from-concept-to-competitive-issue/)
**Author:** Larry Pruss (AI practice lead, SRM)
**Published:** 2026-04-15

## Key stats

- McKinsey projection: Agentic commerce will reach **$3–5T globally by 2030**; **$1T in US retail alone**
- Stablecoin payment activity: **more than doubled in 2025**, reaching **$390–400 billion**
- AI-assisted purchasing: **1 in 6 Black Friday purchases** (2025) involved AI assistance
- AI-driven clicks generate materially better conversion than traditional search

## Core argument

Agentic payments represent a **structural market change**, not a feature. The shift from "AI informs decisions" to "AI executes decisions" means checkout becomes a programmable step in a machine-executed workflow.

## Strategic imperatives for banks

Banks and credit unions must position as the **permissioning and trust layer** between customer intent and autonomous execution — not become invisible funding sources in someone else's ecosystem.

**Six concrete actions:**
1. Develop explicit agentic payments point of view and strategy
2. Invest in permissioning layers: transaction caps, merchant category restrictions, time-bound authority, audit trails
3. Prepare for dual credential world (cards + stablecoins)
4. Modernize fraud models for trusted agent identification
5. Redesign merchant experience for agent readiness
6. Treat 2026–2028 as strategic priority, not innovation experiment

## Dual credential framework

Cards remain. Stablecoins/digital asset wallets become baseline for programmable contexts. Not cards vs. stablecoins — "which credential belongs in which delegated payment context."

## Fraud model shift

Legacy rule "human=good, bot=bad" fails in agentic world. New trust signals needed:
- Agent identity
- Permission scope
- Transaction intent
- Wallet provenance
- Runtime integrity
- Human override rights

## Merchant experience evolution

Anti-bot controls (CAPTCHA, session anomaly detection, anti-scraping) designed to block account takeover now risk blocking legitimate agent purchases — false positives become new business risk.
