# BVNK

**One-line:** Enterprise stablecoin payments infrastructure for global businesses — 4,600+ customers, 130+ countries, licensed across US/UK/EU/Spain — being acquired by Mastercard for $1.8B.

## What they're building

BVNK provides the compliance, custody, and settlement stack that lets enterprises offer stablecoin payments without building the infrastructure themselves. Two models:

1. **Managed Payments** — BVNK handles licensing, custody, compliance; customers launch quickly
2. **Layer1** — Customers bring own licenses/custodian/liquidity; BVNK provides the underlying stablecoin network; enables enterprises to run their own stablecoin payment network in-house

The Layer1 product is the most strategically interesting: major payment processors (Worldpay) can offer stablecoin rails as a native feature of their own product stack.

## Relevance to agentic money

BVNK is the enterprise B2B stablecoin layer. As agent-initiated payments scale, the settlement infrastructure needs to be licensed, compliant, and globally reachable. BVNK provides that backbone — already used by:

- **Worldpay** — stablecoin payouts for marketplaces, travel, gaming
- **dLocal** — cross-border payment acceleration
- **Deel** — freelancer payouts in stablecoins in 100+ countries

## Acquisition: Mastercard ($1.8B)

[[mastercard]] acquiring BVNK for $1.8B. Strategic rationale: BVNK's enterprise stablecoin stack + Mastercard's network = a licensed, globally distributed stablecoin payment layer inside the world's second-largest card network.

This is the most significant traditional finance acquisition of a stablecoin infrastructure company to date.
