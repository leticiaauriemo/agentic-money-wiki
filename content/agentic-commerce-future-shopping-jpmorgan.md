# Agentic Commerce: The Future of AI-Powered Shopping — J.P. Morgan

**Source:** [J.P. Morgan Payments](https://www.jpmorgan.com/payments/newsroom/agentic-commerce-ai-future-shopping)
**Published:** 2026-02-01

## Core argument

Most current "agentic commerce" is NOT truly autonomous. Today's implementations are agent-embedded shopping (similar to social commerce) — agents assist and present, humans approve and pay. True autonomous shopping (agents complete purchases without human approval) will take much longer to scale.

## Evolution of agentic shopping

**Phase 1 (early models):** Discovery only — agents search and compare, humans buy.
**Phase 2 (web-crawling):** Agents pull data from web pages; fragile, easily broken by site changes.
**Phase 3 (current):** Direct integrations — merchants expose structured APIs for agents.

## What merchants need to prepare

1. **Clean, accessible, rich product data** optimized for agent discovery (not human SEO)
2. **Structured product APIs** that agents can query without scraping
3. **Visibility into agent-driven activity** to manage risk appropriately
4. **Post-sale data standards** — agents need to track delivery, returns, disputes

## Open questions JPMorgan identifies

- What defines **consumer consent** in human-not-present transactions?
- What happens when agents **misinterpret user intent**?
- What are **data use standards** across the ecosystem?

## J.P. Morgan's role

Positioning itself as support layer for merchants whether they host own agents or distribute through third-party consumer agents. This connects to JPMorgan Kinexys infrastructure for the settlement layer.

## Timeline realism

"This took 20+ years to build for digital commerce; agentic commerce will evolve faster but won't be at scale tomorrow."
