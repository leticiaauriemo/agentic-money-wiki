# Santander and Mastercard: Europe's First Live AI Agent Payment

**Source:** [Mastercard Press Release, 2026-02-27](https://www.mastercard.com/news/europe/en/newsroom/press-releases/en/2026/)

## What happened

Europe's first live end-to-end payment executed by an AI agent, within Santander's **regulated payment infrastructure** — not a sandbox or demo environment. The transaction was processed through Santander's live payments infrastructure to validate the operational and control framework under real conditions.

The agent initiated and executed a purchase using existing payment networks while maintaining security, privacy, and consumer protection standards. Conducted within Santander's regulated framework — not yet a commercial rollout.

## Santander context

- As of Q3 2025: €1.3 trillion in total funds, 178 million customers, 7,400 branches, 201,000 employees
- Founded 1857, headquartered in Spain
- PayOS (partner) was involved in the implementation

## Technology used

- **Mastercard Agent Pay** — full platform for AI agent-initiated transactions

## Significance

This is the first confirmed live transaction (not pilot, not test environment) of an AI agent completing a payment within a major European regulated bank's actual payment infrastructure. The distinction "within Santander's regulated payment framework" matters — it means AML, fraud, and compliance checks all ran in production conditions.
