# Marketplace — x402scan

**Source:** [x402scan.com/resources](https://www.x402scan.com/resources)

## What it is

The marketplace/resources section of x402scan — the live explorer for the x402 payment protocol ecosystem. Lists and categorizes all merchants and servers accepting x402 payments.

## Server categories

- **AI Servers:** 727 listed
- **Crypto Servers:** 821 listed
- **Trading Info Servers:** 303 listed
- **Search Servers:** 344 listed

## Key observations

- Many servers are unnamed test endpoints (ngrok/Railway URLs) — rapid experimentation happening
- Volume is concentrated: ATXP alone accounts for 12,600+ requests
- Diverse fee structures: some near-zero cost, others high-volume
- Niche services emerging: Padel Maps (sports courts), Clash of Coins (gaming), Xona (creative agents)

## Significance

The marketplace is the closest thing to a "yellow pages" for x402-accessible APIs. Its growth (54 new merchants in a recent period) indicates real developer adoption beyond testing.
