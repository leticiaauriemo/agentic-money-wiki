# Introducing the Universal Cart and More Ways to Help You Shop

**Source:** Vidhya Srinivasan, Google Blog, 2026-05-19
**URL:** https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/google-shopping-cart/

## What's announced

At Google I/O 2026, Google announced the **Universal Cart** — an intelligent, cross-merchant, cross-surface shopping cart built on UCP and Google Wallet.

### Universal Cart features

- Works across Search, Gemini app, YouTube, and Gmail (YouTube and Gmail rolling out after initial launch)
- Runs on Gemini models — gets smarter as models improve
- **Proactive price tracking:** finds deals, price drops, price history, back-in-stock alerts automatically
- **Intelligent reasoning:** flags product incompatibilities (e.g. RAM incompatible with a motherboard in the same cart)
- **Payment-aware:** understands payment method perks, loyalty info, merchant offers via Google Wallet
- **Checkout options:** Google Pay (few taps) or transfer items to merchant's site. Brand stays merchant of record either way.

**Confirmed launch merchants:** Nike, Sephora, Target, Ulta Beauty, Walmart, Wayfair, and Shopify merchants including Fenty and Steve Madden.

**Rollout:** US first (Search + Gemini), summer 2026. YouTube and Gmail to follow.

### UCP expansion

- **New geographies:** Canada and Australia in coming months, then UK
- **New surfaces:** YouTube (US) coming soon
- **New verticals:** Hotel booking and local food delivery coming soon
- New tech partners welcomed to steer the open standard (link to GitHub discussion)

### AP2 launch into Google products

AP2 coming to Google products in the coming months, starting with **Gemini Spark**. Under the hood:
- Creates a transparent, verifiable link between user, merchant, and payment processor
- Privacy-preserving technology keeps user data safe
- Tamper-proof digital mandates — agent always acts on user's behalf
- Permanent digital paper trail for returns and disputes — user and merchant see the same record

## Significance

This article confirms UCP + AP2 are no longer just protocols in spec — they're shipping into Google consumer products used by billions. Universal Cart is the first mass-market agentic commerce interface. The decision to keep the brand as merchant of record (regardless of checkout path) is a key trust signal for merchants who feared losing brand relationship.

The Gemini Spark + AP2 combination is the first confirmation that AP2 has a concrete product launch timeline, not just partner agreements.
